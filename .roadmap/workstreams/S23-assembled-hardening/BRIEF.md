# S23 — Security, Recovery, and Assembled-System Proof

Depends on S18, S20, S21, and S22. Complete injection, authorization escalation, redaction,
credential separation, action reconciliation, cancellation, lease, stale-worker, resource-limit,
partial-provider, and partial-reviewer suites. Verify all three image targets, Compose,
health/readiness, non-root execution, termination, cleanup, trace redaction, and one assembled
fixture path across context, Genkit, Slack, coding job, publication, and projection. Finish recovery,
rotation, compatibility, configuration, and verified-versus-mocked documentation. Exclude production
orchestrator manifests, automatic merge/deploy, unrestricted shell, and recursive spawning.

Also prove the domain-authority dogma end to end: admitted developers receive the complete domain
corpus; non-admitted identities receive none; Git wins conflicts with supplemental memory; Mongo
knowledge can be rebuilt from an exact Git SHA; staging and active revisions never mix; and
transport or runner choice cannot promote content. Validate the projection storage boundary with
deterministic staging, failure, stale-job, rollback, and reconciliation fixtures. The kit does not
claim a provider publisher endpoint or workflow; platform mapping and organization-owned publisher
choices remain the S24 adoption documentation.
