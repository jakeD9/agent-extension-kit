# S18 — Approval and Real Publication Boundary

Depends on S13 and S17. Bind approvals to action, run, repository, revision, capabilities, policy
fingerprint, and expiry. Add one configured Git-provider adapter for supervisor-owned branch,
draft-PR, and review publication, reconciling uncertain remote outcomes before retry. Never expose
merge/deploy/production mutation or publication credentials to a model harness or verification
environment. Verify invalidation, idempotency, least privilege, stale revisions, credential
separation, and opt-in real-provider smoke tests.
